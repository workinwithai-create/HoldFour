# HoldFour

Four-bar live hold desk for bedroom producers and AI-music finishers.

Suno and loop DAWs keep reprinting the same four bars or slam into the next section with no air. Session players write four live bars of hold — pedal, pad sit, half-time, kit thin, bass sit, brass hold — so the unfinished section finally has room to breathe. Then the next section hits with space.

Live samples only. FluidR3 acoustic grand, upright bass, nylon guitar, trumpet, violin, and the PreEight acoustic kit. Loaded from the public PreEight sample CDN. No oscillators. Audio never leaves the tab.

Yesterday’s ship was WalkFour (live walking bass under an unfinished chorus). HoldFour is the missing **hold** so a section does not collapse.

## Loop

1. Pick a groove (Amber Room, Porch Light, Fold Night).
2. Pick a hold (pedal, pad sit, half-time, kit thin, bass sit, nylon air, brass hold, violin line, stop hold, full hold).
3. Hear the section loop without the hold.
4. Hear the full pass: four bars of section, then four bars of hold.
5. Copy the punch list and bounce a WAV of bars 5–8.
